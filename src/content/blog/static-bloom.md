A clean timing report is a quiet thing. No red paths, no violated constraints, just slack where slack should be.

The relationship between arrival time and required time is simple:

$$
slack = T_{required} - T_{arrival}
$$

Everything else is bookkeeping.

When the number is positive, the design breathes. When it is negative, the weekend disappears.
